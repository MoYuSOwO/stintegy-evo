# StintegyEVO – Race Strategy Engineer Simulator

[中文](README_zh.md)

**StintegyEVO** is a **physics-driven race strategy simulation game**. You don't play as the driver — you are the **race engineer**. Your job is to command AI drivers to execute your tactics during races, qualifying, and endurance events by adjusting strategies like **torque distribution, energy recovery, and tire thermal management** in real time, then witness the consequences of those decisions in a highly realistic physics environment.

> **Note: You don't need a gamepad or steering wheel.** All driving is performed by AI drivers. You only issue strategy commands.

---

## 🚀 Core Features

### 1. Modern Electric Race Car Physics Engine
Not a simple accelerate/decelerate model, but a **complete four-motor independent drive physics system**:

*   **Four-Wheel Independent Torque Vectoring**: Front/rear axle power distribution, independent torque control for inside/outside wheels through corners, enabling real-time adjustment of vehicle handling characteristics.
*   **Pacejka Magic Formula Tire Model**: Both lateral and longitudinal forces generated by classic Pacejka curves, with combined slip (friction ellipse) constraints.
*   **Dual-Layer Tire Thermodynamics**: Surface temperature (friction heating), core temperature (rolling heating), dynamic tire pressure, and wear calculated from slip power.
*   **Blended Braking System**: Regenerative braking and mechanical brake backup unified under a single-pedal logic, with battery SOC protection and full-charge decoupling.
*   **Load Transfer & Suspension**: Dynamic four-wheel independent load based on longitudinal/lateral acceleration, including downforce distribution.

### 2. AI Strategy Execution (Planned)
Your AI drivers will:
*   **Speed Management**: Automatically control acceleration and braking based on track speed limits and preview distance.
*   **Traction Control**: Monitor tire slip ratio in real time, proactively adjusting power output to avoid excessive wheelspin.
*   **Racecraft**: Overtaking, defending, DRS/Attack Mode management.
*   **Stylization**: Generate drivers with different driving styles through perception noise and assertiveness parameters.

### 3. Strategy Depth
Adjust in real time during a race:
*   Front/rear axle drive force distribution
*   Torque vectoring strength (cornering agility vs. tire wear)
*   Regenerative braking intensity (energy saving vs. corner entry stability)
*   Motor target temperature / power ceiling
*   Slipstream & dirty air博弈 (downforce loss)

### 4. Open Source & Extensible
*   Physics engine fully self-developed, no third-party physics middleware.
*   Track data based on curve definitions, easy for the community to create new tracks.
*   **AGPLv3 + CLA** dual licensing — contributions welcome while protecting commercial licensing prospects.

## 🧠 Design Philosophy

Racing games usually make you the driver. But in real motorsport, races are often won and lost on the Pit Wall. StintegyEVO wants you to experience being **the person holding the strategy clipboard**. You don't need zero-second reaction times — but you do need to understand the博弈 between tires, motors, battery charge, and aerodynamics.

### 🆚 How StintegyEVO Differs from Other Racing Games

| Dimension | Motorsport Manager | F1 Manager 2024 | iRacing | **StintegyEVO** |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Positioning** | Team management sim | Official F1 team management | Hardcore driving sim | **Race Strategy Engineer Simulator** |
| **Physics Engine** | Simplified numerical model | Game-level physics | Industrial-grade (NTMv7) | **Self-developed EV physics engine** |
| **Powertrain** | Traditional ICE | F1 hybrid (abstracted) | ICE / Hybrid | **Modern electric race car** |
| **Driving Method** | AI auto-drive | AI auto-drive | Player drives | **AI executes, player issues strategy commands** |
| **Strategy Depth** | Pit stops / tires / engine modes | Pit stops / tires / ERS | On-track competition | **Torque distribution, energy recovery, motor temp management, tire thermodynamics, slipstream & dirty air** |
| **Tire Model** | Simplified wear | Game-level | Industrial-grade (NTMv7) | **Pacejka Magic Formula + dual-layer temperature + dynamic tire pressure** |
| **Extensibility** | Mod support | Limited | Closed ecosystem | **Open Source (AGPLv3)** |

### Why Can't Existing Games Do What StintegyEVO Does?

- **Traditional driving simulators (iRacing/ACC/Forza) make you the driver.** StintegyEVO makes you the race engineer.
- **Motorsport management games (MM/F1M) have strategy management, but their physics are numerical approximations.** You can't adjust torque vectoring or see dual-layer tire temperatures — because they don't have a real physics engine underneath.
- **StintegyEVO sits at the intersection of both:** A strategy management simulation powered by an industrial-grade physics engine. The tactical depth of *Motorsport Manager* × the engineering rigor of EV powertrain physics. This is the only race strategy game on the market where every strategic decision is driven by a self-developed physics engine.

## 🗺️ Roadmap

StintegyEVO is a solo-developed project, currently in the **core physics & AI validation phase**. The roadmap below shows completed work, tasks in progress, and future plans. If a particular direction interests you, feel free to jump in and help push it forward — the AGPLv3 license and CLA ensure your contributions are properly protected while the project retains healthy commercialization potential.

### ✅ Completed (v0.1 – v0.5, The Core Physics Era)

- **Tire Physics Model**
  - Pacejka lateral and longitudinal force curves.
  - Friction ellipse combined constraints, natural competition between longitudinal/lateral grip.
  - Dual-layer temperature (surface temp, core temp) and dynamic tire pressure.
  - Slip-power-based wear calculation, supporting independent longitudinal/lateral wear coefficients.

- **EV Powertrain & Energy Management**
  - Four-motor independent drive architecture with torque vectoring control (front/rear distribution, inside/outside wheel distribution through corners).
  - Single-pedal logic: drive, regenerative braking, and mechanical brake backup unified.
  - Battery SOC tracking, automatic regen cutoff at full charge to prevent overcharging.
  - Independently adjustable regenerative braking intensity.

- **Chassis & Aerodynamics**
  - Dynamic load transfer (longitudinal/lateral acceleration), four-wheel independent load calculation.
  - Front/rear downforce distribution, dirty air factor (downforce loss in slipstream).
  - Suspension damping smoothing to prevent numerical oscillation from abrupt load changes.

- **Partial AI Foundation**
  - Dynamic path generation (cosine regression racing line).
  - Track-curvature-based speed planning (forward speed limiting).

### 🚧 In Progress (v0.6, Stabilization & Deepening)

- **Motor Thermal Management** – Introduce motor temperature model with overheat power output limiting; adds strategy dimension.
- **Steering System Fix** – Implement Ackermann geometry (independent inside/outside wheel steering angles), combined with speed-sensitive steering ratio to resolve inside tire excessive wear and overheating.
- **Load Transfer Direction Fix** – Correct sign error in lateral load transfer formula to ensure outside/inside tire temperatures and wear match physical intuition.
- **Track Slope & Banking Support** – Slope affects longitudinal acceleration, banking affects cornering centripetal force; track data structure expansion.
- **AI Controller** – Optimize parameters or model so AI can complete a lap like a real racing driver and respond to player strategy parameters.

### 📋 Short-Term Plans (v0.7 – v0.8, From "Physics Engine" to "Playable Race")

- **AI Racecraft Logic**
  - Overtaking: identify lead car, calculate pull-out timing, dynamic path offset.
  - Defending: inside/outside line selection, adjust aggression based on remaining tire life and battery charge.
  - Attack Mode / DRS simulation.
- **Driver Style System**
  - Perception noise (grip, speed, distance perception bias).
  - Assertiveness slider (braking timing), affecting lap time and consistency.
- **Race Weekend Framework**
  - Full flow of practice, qualifying, and race (with fuel/battery management).
  - Simple tire selection and setup presets.

### 🔭 Mid-Term Vision (v0.9 – v1.0, Polishing to a Finished Product)

- **Low Poly 3D Visuals** – Godot-based 3D rendering layer paired with 2D physics logic, crafting an *Art of Rally*-style low-poly aesthetic.
- **Basic UI & Telemetry Panel** – Real-time display of tire temperatures, motor status, battery SOC, lap time comparison, and other core data.
- **Track Editor Prototype** – Players can create their own tracks; AI automatically calculates preview and speed limits without offline baking.
- **Full Strategy Console** – Slider interface for real-time in-race adjustment of torque distribution, regen intensity, motor target temperature, rear wing angle, etc.

### 🌟 Long-Term Vision (v1.x+, Community Imagination)

- **Multiplayer Mode** – Multiple players each commanding their own teams competing simultaneously online.
- **Advanced Race Simulation** – Safety car, virtual safety car, dynamic weather, track surface temperature, and standing water.
- **Career Mode** – Full team management, R&D tree, driver market.
- **Hardware-in-the-Loop Interface** – Connect to real vehicle data acquisition systems or export the engine to professional simulation software (reserving possibility for engineering applications).
